# Machine Learning in Physics

## Fall 2026

Course materials for **Machine Learning in Physics**.

The course introduces modern machine-learning methods with emphasis on applications to physical problems. It combines lectures with hands-on laboratory classes implemented mainly in Python and PyTorch.

---

## Lecture notes

1. Machine learning fundamentals
2. Deep neural networks
3. Convolutional neural networks
4. Recurrent neural networks
5. Attention and transformers
6. Autoencoders and generative models
7. Graph neural networks
8. Group-equivariant neural networks
9. Physics-informed neural networks
10. Self- and semi-supervised learning

## Laboratory classes

The laboratories introduce machine-learning methods through numerical experiments and problems motivated by physics.

### 1. Preliminary problems — neural-network fundamentals
- simple perceptron networks
- gradient descent and backpropagation
- Universal Approximation Theorem
- PyTorch basics

[Lab 1 →](labs/lab01/)

### 2. Image classification
- MNIST classification
- perceptron, deep fully-connected networks and CNNs
- overfitting, regularization and early stopping
- data augmentation

[Lab 2 →](labs/lab02/)

### 3. ECG signal classification
- feature engineering
- PCA
- SVM
- decision trees
- random forests
- scikit-learn

[Lab 3 →](labs/lab03/)

### 4. Group-equivariant CNNs
- symmetries in physical systems
- group actions
- equivariant convolutional networks
- applications to crystal structures

[Lab 4 →](labs/lab04/)

### 5. Physics-informed neural networks
- differential equations and neural networks
- automatic differentiation
- physics-informed loss functions
- solving differential equations with PINNs

[Lab 5 →](labs/lab05/)

### 6. Transformer encoder
- attention and self-attention
- positional encoding
- transformer encoder
- applications to physical data

[Lab 6 →](labs/lab06/)
